Fábrica Apps – Privacy Policy Effective Date: July 27, 2026 Thank you for using applications published by Fábrica Apps ("we", "our", or "us"). Your privacy is important to us. Our goal is to build simple, privacy-friendly mobile applications that work primarily offline and collect as little personal information as possible. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use any mobile application published under the Fábrica Apps developer account on Google Play or the Apple App Store. 1. Scope This Privacy Policy applies to all mobile applications developed and published by Fábrica Apps unless a specific application contains its own supplemental privacy notice. Examples include, but are not limited to: BuildProfit Debt Crush Divide n Eat Envelope Budget FitPro Velora Memora Some applications may include additional disclosures when required by law or when specific features are introduced. 2. Our Privacy Philosophy Our applications are designed around the following principles: Privacy by Design Offline First Minimal Data Collection No Account Required (unless explicitly stated) No Sale of Personal Information Whenever possible, your information remains stored locally on your own device. 3. Information We Collect Most Fábrica Apps applications do not require: user registration usernames passwords phone numbers home addresses government identification payment card information photographs contacts The majority of user-generated content remains stored locally on your device. 4. Information Automatically Collected Some applications may automatically collect limited technical information through trusted third-party services. This information may include: Device model Operating system version App version Language Country or region Anonymous identifiers Crash reports Performance information This information is used exclusively to improve application quality, reliability and stability. 5. Local Storage Most information created inside our applications is stored locally on your device. Examples include: budgets calculations project estimates timers workout logs saved preferences application settings We generally do not receive or store this information on our servers. If you uninstall the application, locally stored information may be permanently deleted unless you have created your own backup. 6. Information collected by Google may include: Advertising Identifier Device Information Approximate Location (if enabled by the operating system) Advertising Performance Data For more information, please review Google's Privacy Policy. Purchasing the "Remove Ads" option disables advertising within the application where applicable. 7. In-App Purchases Some applications offer optional purchases such as: Remove Ads Lifetime Premium Monthly Subscription Annual Subscription Purchases are securely processed by: Google Play Billing Apple In-App Purchases We do not receive or store your payment card information.
